    About Deutschraum

    A school club for first German.

    Deutschraum is a student-led school club that takes beginners from zero German to everyday A1 conversations — alphabet, greetings, people, school, food, and town — in short lessons with flashcards and quizzes. This file is the proposed design and structure for the club team to refine.

    Learning goals

    Read, listen, write, speak.

    Every chapter feeds the four skills: short readings, listen-and-repeat lines, small writing tasks, and speaking frames like “Ich heiße …”. Revision is built into the path, not bolted on.
