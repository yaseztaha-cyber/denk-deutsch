About Deutschraum

A school club that makes German feel easy.

Deutschraum is a student-led school club that teaches beginners German at A1 level — from the alphabet and first greetings to everyday conversations. The path is organised into small modules with short lessons, flashcards, and quizzes. This prototype shows the proposed design and structure for the club team to refine.
